Drug Education

Understand drugs, their effects and the risks of substance abuse.

Know the facts

  • Alcohol can affect judgement and coordination.
  • Tobacco and nicotine can cause addiction.
  • Drug misuse can affect physical and mental wellbeing.
  • Seeking help early can make recovery easier.

Peer Pressure Guidance

You have the right to make your own decisions.

  • Learn to say NO confidently.
  • Choose friends who respect your decisions.
  • Walk away from unsafe situations.
  • Talk to someone you trust when under pressure.
